Portal

The platform an entire remote team's job lived on, designed and built by one person.

Leverage Homes piloted a new sales structure: remote appointment setters feeding appointments to in-house closers. The RPEC Portal was where that job happened, a Salesforce Experience Cloud platform for setting appointments, creating opportunities, and logging notes, wired into Salesforce so every appointment routed to the right closer, accurately, automatically.

I designed and built the entire platform

The pilot needed its tooling fast, and it needed the tooling to be right: a remote setter's entire workday would happen inside it, and every appointment they set had to land with the right closer in Salesforce without anyone checking behind it. I owned all of it.

Designed

The experience

The full interface for a job that lived on screen: setting appointments, creating new opportunities, adding notes, and seeing the day's work clearly. Built for new remote hires to learn fast and use all day.

Constructed on Salesforce Experience Cloud, from the portal itself through the custom functionality the pilot's workflow needed.

The outcome

The platform did its job. The business hypothesis didn't, and leadership sunset the pilot.

The remote-setter model didn't perform the way leadership projected, and the program was eventually halted. The portal itself ran as designed: appointments flowed, routing held up, and the tooling was never the bottleneck. Building something that works for a bet that doesn't is part of shipping real products, and this project is where I learned that lesson firsthand.
