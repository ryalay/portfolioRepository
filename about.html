About

Hello! My name is Ryan and I'm a UX designer from New Jersey. I studied at Rutgers University, where I graduated with honors in my bachelor's degree in Information Technology and Informatics, with a web design pathway. I also completed a double minor in Business Administration and Digital Communication, Information, & Media.

My eye for design and attention to detail have developed with me since I was a kid, when I was customizing and tinkering with the software on devices around the house. Now, combined with my love for solving problems, I'm devoted to creating digital experiences that are impactful, attractive, and intuitive.

When I'm not designing, I am an avid skier, and love traveling. I also like surfing and enjoy going to concerts and music festivals.
