About me

Hello! I'm Ryan, a UX designer based in the NYC metro area. I studied at Rutgers University, where I graduated with honors in my bachelor's degree in Information Technology and Informatics, with a web design pathway. I also completed a double minor in Business Administration and Digital Communication, Information, & Media.

My eye for design and attention to detail have developed with me since I was a kid, when I was customizing and tinkering with the software on devices around the house. Now, combined with my love for solving problems, I'm devoted to creating digital experiences that are impactful, attractive, and intuitive.

When I'm not designing, I am an avid skier, and love traveling. I also like surfing and enjoy going to concerts and music festivals.

Staying curious

95% of the ocean is unseen by the human eye.

I've always liked this fact, and it's a great reminder that, even when it might not seem like it, there’s always so much more out there to learn and explore.

So, every day I stay curious.

Whether I'm working or on vacation, I love the feeling of finding new solutions to problems, learning a new skill, or exploring a new place.
